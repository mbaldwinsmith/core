# Quotations and source register

These short, source-checked extracts are a starting quotation bank, not a substitute for context. The wording follows the linked English editions; an older text and a modern translation have different rights histories. Some online patristic editions explicitly include editorial revisions. Before public release, establish permissions or replace the wording with a verified, suitably reusable edition. Quoted material is not automatically covered by the proposed open licence for original course writing. Do not attribute anonymous internet sayings to a saint.

| Q1 | “You have made us for Yourself, and our hearts are restless until they rest in You.” | Augustine, Confessions I.1.1, translated by J. G. Pilkington, in Nicene and Post Nicene Fathers, first series, volume 1; New Advent edition. An invocation to God, not a promise that faith removes all anxiety. [Source](https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/110101.htm) |
| Q2 | “the encounter with an event, a person” | Benedict XVI, Deus caritas est 1, official English text. A short excerpt about the origin of Christian life. [Source](https://www.vatican.va/content/benedict-xvi/en/encyclicals/documents/hf_ben-xvi_enc_20051225_deus-caritas-est.html) |
| Q3 | “in communion, in intimacy, with Jesus Christ” | Saint John Paul II, Catechesi tradendae 5, official English text. A phrase identifying catechesis' aim. [Source](https://www.vatican.va/content/john-paul-ii/en/apost_exhortations/documents/hf_jp-ii_exh_16101979_catechesi-tradendae.html) |
| Q4 | “For He was made man that we might be made God” | Athanasius, On the Incarnation 54.3, translated by Archibald Robertson, Nicene and Post Nicene Fathers, second series, volume 4; New Advent edition. Explain participation by grace, not becoming another divine being or sharing God's essence by nature. [Source](https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/2802.htm) |
| Q5 | “Through the Holy Spirit comes our restoration to paradise” | Basil of Caesarea, On the Holy Spirit 15.36, translated by Blomfield Jackson, Nicene and Post Nicene Fathers, second series, volume 8; New Advent edition. Read as an account of the Spirit's saving gifts. [Source](https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/3203.htm) |
| Q6 | “wherever Jesus Christ is, there is the Catholic Church” | Ignatius of Antioch, Letter to the Smyrnaeans 8, translated by Alexander Roberts and James Donaldson, Ante Nicene Fathers, volume 1; New Advent edition. Early witness to Christ, Eucharist and episcopal communion; not a standalone proof of every later ecclesiological formulation. [Source](https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0109.htm) |
| Q7 | “The Pope is not an absolute monarch whose thoughts and desires are law.” | Benedict XVI, homily at Saint John Lateran, 7 May 2005, official English text. Read with his explanation that teaching authority serves God's Word. [Source](https://www.vatican.va/content/benedict-xvi/en/homilies/2005/documents/hf_ben-xvi_hom_20050507_san-giovanni-laterano.html) |
| Q8 | “For me, prayer is a surge of the heart” | Thérèse of Lisieux, Manuscript C 25r, as quoted in CCC 2558. The extract introduces a description embracing trial as well as joy. [Source](https://www.vatican.va/content/catechism/en/part_four/section_one.html) |
| Q9 | “the knot of Eve's disobedience was loosed by the obedience of Mary” | Irenaeus, Against Heresies III.22.4, translated by Alexander Roberts and William Rambaut, Ante Nicene Fathers, volume 1; New Advent edition. Explain Mary's grace-enabled response in relation to Christ, not an independent source of redemption. [Source](https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0103322.htm) |
| Q10 | “a close sharing between friends” | Teresa of Ávila, Life 8.5, as quoted in CCC 2709. A brief phrase from her account of prayer; not a test that every prayer must feel emotionally intimate. [Source](https://www.vatican.va/content/catechism/en/part_four/section_one/chapter_three/article_1/iii_contemplative_prayer.html) |
| Q11 | “Our prayer is public and common” | Cyprian, On the Lord's Prayer 8, translated by Robert Ernest Wallis, Ante Nicene Fathers, volume 5; New Advent edition. The context explains why Jesus teaches “our,” rather than only individual petitions. [Source](https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/050704.htm) |
| Q12 | “seeing is far more persuasive than hearing” | Mystagogical Catechesis I.1, transmitted under Cyril of Jerusalem; Catechetical Lecture 19 in the linked edition, translated by Edwin Hamilton Gifford, Nicene and Post Nicene Fathers, second series, volume 7. Attribution of the mystagogical lectures is discussed by scholars; identify the textual tradition rather than claiming certainty beyond it. The original addresses the newly baptised. [Source](https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/310119.htm) |
| Q13 | “For not as common bread and common drink do we receive these” | Justin Martyr, First Apology 66, translated by Marcus Dods and George Reith, Ante Nicene Fathers, volume 1; New Advent edition. Read alongside his account of Christ's flesh and blood and Sunday worship in chapters 65–67. [Source](https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0126.htm) |
| Q14 | “Jesus Christ is the face of the Father's mercy.” | Francis, Misericordiae vultus 1, official English text; apostrophe normalised typographically. The course draws from the document, not from a prediction of any individual's spiritual or medical outcome. [Source](https://www.vatican.va/content/francesco/en/bulls/documents/papa-francesco_bolla_20150411_misericordiae-vultus.html) |
| Q15 | “Christian, recognize your dignity” | Saint Leo the Great, Sermon 21.3, as quoted in CCC 1691. Read with the baptismal summons to live accordingly. [Source](https://www.vatican.va/content/catechism/en/part_three.index.html) |

**How to build the completed bank.** Each session already has at least one assigned excerpt. Add further quotations only where they clarify a point; do not force a Father, a saint and a pope into every meeting. Across the full course ensure chronological and geographical breadth. Log author, work, section, date where known, translation, edition, URL, access date, exact wording, omissions, doctrinal context and rights status. Record all quotations checked here as accessed on 27 October 2026. Proposed future quotations remain unapproved until checked in a primary text. An individual Father's statement is not automatically identical with a later dogmatic definition; some Fathers disagreed or held views the Church did not receive.

**Primary references.** R0 is the Catechism navigation point; other entries identify the principal source families for the writer. Session-specific paragraph ranges are the controlling locators. Use the site contents to navigate if a page address changes. A direct article landing page may show only the opening paragraph; its linked subsections contain the rest of the named range.
