# Resurrection Christian hope and continuing discipleship

## Begin with a question

What are we hoping for, and how do we go on? Participants can distinguish resurrection, heaven, purgatory, hell and final judgment, and choose a realistic next step after the course.

## Explore the faith

1. Christian hope includes the resurrection of the body and a renewed creation, not merely escape into a disembodied existence.

2. After death comes particular judgment; heaven is communion with God, purgatory the final purification of those assured of salvation, and hell definitive self-exclusion from communion with God. Final judgment manifests God's justice and the consequences of our lives.

3. Christ will return in glory to judge the living and the dead; his return is linked to the resurrection and final judgment, completing his kingdom rather than beginning another ordinary earthly political reign (CCC 668–682). Hope calls us to prayer, conversion, service and witness now. Do not predict dates, declare particular people damned or invent assurances about individuals; distinguish this restraint from the Church’s recognition of canonised saints in glory.

## Read and discover

1 Corinthians 15:20–28, 42–49; Revelation 21:1–5; Matthew 28:16–20; CCC 668–682, 988–1065, 1817–1821; Q1 Augustine, returning to rest in God. References R28 and R0.

## Questions worth asking

### Is purgatory a second chance to reject or accept God?

No; it is purification of those who die in God's grace.

### Can we know that a particular person is in hell?

The course should not pronounce such judgments; entrust people to God's justice and mercy.

### Does hope make present suffering unimportant?

No; resurrection gives bodily life and acts of love lasting significance.

## Reflect and practise

Spend 5 minutes making a Creed map from memory with support, 5 clarifying one outstanding question and 5 choosing a next step in prayer, community or service. Expected output: a one-month discipleship plan and a course feedback form. Exit check: explain resurrection, Christ’s glorious return and why purgatory differs from hell.

## Continue the journey

Offer an optional reunion. Those exploring initiation meet the parish team; others can join a Scripture group, prayer group or service activity. Avoid presenting religious life, ordination or marriage as an expected course outcome.
