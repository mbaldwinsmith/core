# Holy Orders and the service of communion

## Begin with a question

How does ordained ministry serve the whole Church? Participants can distinguish bishop, priest and deacon, and describe how Holy Orders relates to everyone's baptismal vocation.

## Explore the faith

1. Holy Orders continues the apostolic ministry in three degrees: episcopate, presbyterate and diaconate.

2. Bishops and priests share ministerial priesthood; deacons are ordained for service, including word, liturgy and charity, rather than ordained to the priesthood.

3. The essential sign is the bishop's laying on of hands with the consecratory prayer. It confers grace and an enduring character; the sacrament does not make someone morally superior or above accountability.

## Signs celebration and preparation

Only a validly ordained bishop confers Orders. Catholic teaching reserves sacred ordination to baptised men; there is no personal entitlement to ordination. Explain vocational discernment, formation, episcopal admission and the distinction between sacramental teaching and disciplines such as priestly celibacy. Latin priests are normally celibate; married priests exist in Eastern Catholic Churches and under certain Latin provisions. Bishops are chosen from celibate men. Religious profession and consecrated life are distinct from ordination. Sources: CCC 1536–1600 [R24].

## Read and discover

Mark 10:42–45; 1 Timothy 4:14; 2 Timothy 1:6; Q6 Ignatius for early episcopal communion, read within its historical context.

## Questions worth asking

### Is the ordained priesthood the only vocation?

No; baptismal holiness and mission belong to all, in many states of life.

### Why are women not ordained?

Present the Church's stated teaching and its account of the apostolic pattern and Christ's action, without implying lesser dignity or fewer gifts; allow serious questions.

### Does ordination excuse abuse?

Never; sacramental character does not cancel safeguarding, law or accountability.

## Reflect and practise

Spend 5 minutes matching the three degrees to their ministries, 5 distinguishing Orders from religious vows and 5 mapping ways lay and ordained members collaborate. Expected output: a ministry map. Exit check: explain why a monk is not necessarily a priest and why a deacon cannot consecrate the Eucharist.

## Carry it into the week

Pray for vocations and identify a form of service appropriate to your own life. Optional guest testimony must leave time for questions.
