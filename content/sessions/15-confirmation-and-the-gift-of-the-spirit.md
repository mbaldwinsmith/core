# Confirmation and the gift of the Spirit

## Begin with a question

How are we strengthened for Christian witness? Participants can explain Confirmation's relation to Baptism and identify its essential signs, effects and preparation route.

## Explore the faith

1. Confirmation deepens baptismal grace and strengthens our bond with Christ and the Church for witness.

2. Explain laying on of hands, anointing with sacred chrism and the sacramental words in the relevant approved rite; distinguish the whole ritual from its essential elements.

3. It is received once by a baptised person not yet confirmed. In the Latin Church the bishop is the ordinary minister and priests may confirm under specified faculties; in Eastern Catholic Churches priests normally chrismate with the prescribed chrism. Age and preparation practices differ.

## Signs celebration and preparation

Explain the enduring character, prayer, suitable formation and the sponsor's role. For those with the use of reason, preparation includes understanding, an intention to receive and appropriate disposition; leaders refer individual circumstances to the parish. Sacred chrism is perfumed oil consecrated for sacramental use. Sources: CCC 1285–1321 [R19].

## Read and discover

Acts 8:14–17; Acts 19:1–7; Q5 Basil on the Spirit and adoption.

## Questions worth asking

### Is this graduation?

No; Christian initiation leads into continuing life in Christ.

### Does it mean the Spirit was absent before?

No; the Spirit already acts in Baptism and in the person's life.

### Why do some babies receive it?

Eastern initiation shows that this is God's gift, not a reward for adolescent independence.

## Reflect and practise

Spend 5 minutes comparing a Latin and an Eastern initiation timeline, 5 identifying sacramental signs and 5 drafting a small act of Christian witness. Expected output: an accurately labelled timeline and service commitment. Exit check: explain why “choosing my religion for the first time” is incomplete as a definition.

## Carry it into the week

Ask for courage to carry out one act of faithful service. Show an image or a written description of chrism; do not imitate the sacramental anointing.
