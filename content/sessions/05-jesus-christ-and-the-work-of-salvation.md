# Jesus Christ and the work of salvation

## Begin with a question

Who is Jesus and what does he save us for? Participants can explain that Jesus is one divine Person with a complete divine nature and a complete human nature, and summarise the Paschal mystery.

## Explore the faith

1. The eternal Son truly becomes human; he is neither partly God nor a human person later promoted to divinity.

2. His teaching, healing, obedience, cross and bodily resurrection belong together in God's saving work.

3. His once-for-all self-offering reconciles us to God and opens adopted life in him; no single analogy exhausts the atonement. Teach his real death and burial, descent to the dead, resurrection, Ascension and continuing heavenly intercession as the movement of one saving mystery. Jesus' Jewish identity matters. The passion must never be used to assign collective guilt to Jewish people.

## Read and discover

John 1:1–18; Philippians 2:5–11; 1 Corinthians 15:3–8; CCC 422–478, 595–618, 624–682; Q4 Athanasius. References R0, R6 and Q4's source.

## Questions worth asking

### Did Jesus merely seem human?

No; he truly lived and suffered as man.

### Does the resurrection mean his ideas survived?

Catholic faith proclaims his real bodily resurrection, in a glorified life beyond ordinary resuscitation.

### Did the Father stop loving the Son on the cross?

No; Father and Son are united in the divine saving love, while Christ truly enters human suffering.

## Creed coverage

Explain the descent as Christ truly entering death and bringing salvation to the just who awaited him, not suffering the punishment of the damned (CCC 632–637). Ascension means his glorified humanity enters heavenly glory; seated at the Father’s right hand expresses his reign and continuing intercession, not retirement from human concerns (CCC 659–667). Christ’s glorious return is revisited in Session 24 (CCC 668–682).

## Reflect and practise

Spend 3 minutes correcting “Jesus was half God,” 6 ordering death, descent, resurrection and Ascension, and 6 explaining their meaning in pairs. Expected output: a salvation timeline with one sentence explaining descent and one explaining Ascension and intercession. Exit check: distinguish descent to the dead from the hell of damnation and explain how the risen Christ remains active.

## Pacing note

Keep the salvation timeline central; specialist comparisons of atonement theories belong in optional depth.
