# The Church and belonging to Christ

## Begin with a question

Why a Church rather than solitary belief? Participants can explain the four marks and the relationship between the whole Church, a diocese and a parish.

## Explore the faith

1. The Church is God's people, Christ's body and the Spirit's temple, both visibly structured and a spiritual communion.

2. One, holy, catholic and apostolic name gifts and a calling; holiness does not mean every member or leader acts well.

3. The Catholic Church includes Eastern Catholic Churches. Other Christians share real elements of Christian life; ecumenism seeks unity in truth and charity. Explain the Church's necessity in relation to Christ without claiming all non-Catholics are automatically condemned.

## Read and discover

Acts 2:42–47; 1 Corinthians 12:12–27; CCC 748–870; Lumen gentium 8, 14–16; Q6 Ignatius. References R8, R9, R10.

## Questions worth asking

### Is the Church just its clergy?

No; all the baptised share its life and mission.

### How can a holy Church contain abuse?

Christ's gifts do not excuse human wrongdoing; truth, justice, protection and repentance are required.

### Can other Christians be saved?

Salvation comes through Christ; Catholic teaching recognises God's grace beyond visible Catholic boundaries and entrusts individuals to his judgment.

## Reflect and practise

Spend 5 minutes mapping parish and diocese, 5 linking local ministries to the four marks and 5 identifying a safe route for concerns. Expected output: a simple local Church map with verified contacts. Exit check: explain “catholic” as universal.

## Carry it into the week

Observe a parish activity or read about an Eastern Catholic community. No one is pressured to enter a building or meet a leader they do not feel safe with.
