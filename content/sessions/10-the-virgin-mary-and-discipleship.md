# The Virgin Mary and discipleship

## Begin with a question

Why does Mary matter to faith in Jesus? Participants can explain the four Marian dogmas and distinguish them from optional devotions and private revelations.

## Explore the faith

1. Mother of God affirms that the one Mary bore is truly God the Son incarnate; she is a creature redeemed by Christ, not the source of his divinity.

2. Teach perpetual virginity, Immaculate Conception and Assumption. Immaculate Conception concerns Mary's preservation from original sin from her conception by Christ's merits, not Jesus' virginal conception. The Assumption concerns her being taken body and soul into heavenly glory.

3. Mary's faith and intercession are wholly dependent on Christ; honour for her differs essentially from adoration of God. Rosary, icons and other devotions should lead to Christ.

## Read and discover

Luke 1:26–56; John 2:1–11; CCC 484–511, 963–975; Q9 Irenaeus on Mary and Eve. References R13 and R0.

## Questions worth asking

### Do Catholics worship Mary?

No; adoration belongs to God alone.

### Must I accept an apparition?

Private revelations add nothing to the deposit of faith and are not binding as articles of faith, even when approved.

### Does the Bible call Jesus' relatives brothers?

It does; explain the Catholic reading alongside wider biblical kinship language, acknowledging that other Christians interpret these passages differently.
