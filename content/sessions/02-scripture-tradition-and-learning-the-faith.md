# Scripture Tradition and learning the faith

## Begin with a question

How do we know what Christians believe? Participants can locate a biblical passage and a CCC paragraph and distinguish apostolic Tradition from a changeable custom.

## Explore the faith

1. Revelation is God's self-communication, fulfilled in Christ.

2. Scripture and apostolic Tradition transmit the one deposit of faith; the Church's teaching office serves that Word.

3. Biblical interpretation considers genre, historical setting, the unity of Scripture and the Church's faith. The Old Testament remains Christian Scripture; Israel's story must not be reduced to a discarded preliminary.

## Read and discover

2 Timothy 3:14–17; Luke 24:27; CCC 50–100, 101–141; Dei Verbum 9–12; Q3 John Paul II on the purpose of catechesis. References R0, R1, R3.

## Questions worth asking

### Do Catholics read the Bible?

Yes; Scripture is central to worship and should nourish personal life.

### Is every biblical passage the same kind of history?

No; poetry, parable, law, letters and historical narrative require appropriate reading.

### Can a custom change?

Some disciplines and customs can; the apostolic faith is not simply a collection of local preferences.

## Reflect and practise

With a Bible contents page and a CCC sample, spend 5 minutes locating Luke 15, 5 locating CCC 80 and 5 comparing a biblical text, a doctrinal summary and a local custom. Expected output: a correctly labelled three-item sheet. Exit check: explain why a quotation needs context.

## Carry it into the week

Read Luke 15 and note one question. Offer the passage read aloud so the task does not depend on print literacy.
