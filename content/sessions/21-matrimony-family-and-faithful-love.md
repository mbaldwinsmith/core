# Matrimony family and faithful love

## Begin with a question

What does the Church mean by marriage as a sacrament? Participants can describe covenant, free consent, permanence, fidelity and openness to life, and identify pastoral help for complex situations.

## Explore the faith

1. Marriage is a covenant between a man and a woman ordered to the spouses' good and the generation and education of children; between the baptised a valid marriage is a sacrament.

2. Free consent, the absence of impediments and observance of applicable canonical requirements matter; a wedding ceremony alone does not resolve every question of validity.

3. Teach unity, indissolubility, mutual self-giving, chastity within one's state of life and responsible parenthood. Explain Catholic teaching on the inseparability of the unitive and procreative meanings of marital intercourse and its rejection of contraception, with sensitive, sourced further formation.

## Signs celebration and preparation

In the Latin tradition spouses confer the sacrament on one another through consent before the Church's authorised witness and witnesses, subject to legal exceptions. In Eastern Catholic traditions the priest's blessing has a distinct constitutive role; obtain a qualified review of the relevant rite. Marriage preparation includes freedom, communication, faith and responsibilities. Infertility does not itself invalidate marriage. Sources: CCC 1601–1666, 2360–2379 [R25].

## Read and discover

Genesis 2:18–24; John 2:1–11; Ephesians 5:21–33, explained through mutual self-giving rather than domination; Q2 Benedict XVI on encounter and his wider teaching on love in R2.

## Questions worth asking

### Is an annulment a Catholic divorce?

No; a declaration of nullity judges whether a valid marriage came into being.

### Must someone remain in an unsafe home?

No; protection and separation can be necessary.

### Can divorced people come to church?

Yes; distinguish civil divorce, a subsequent union and individual sacramental questions, which need informed pastoral accompaniment rather than public judgment.

## Reflect and practise

Use 5 minutes to identify free consent in fictional cases, 5 to distinguish permanence from tolerating abuse and 5 to locate preparation or tribunal guidance. Expected output: a safe referral map. Exit check: explain why inability to conceive does not mean inability to marry.

## Carry it into the week

Practise one act of faithful care in a family or friendship. Single, widowed, divorced and LGBTQ participants should be addressed respectfully; nobody's personal circumstances become a debate exercise.
