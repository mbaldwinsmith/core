# Reconciliation mercy and truthful return

## Begin with a question

How do we return after sin? Participants can describe a first or returning confession, distinguish mortal and venial sin, and identify a trusted parish route without disclosing sins to the group.

## Explore the faith

1. Christ gives the Church a sacramental ministry of forgiveness and reconciliation.

2. The penitent's acts are contrition, confession and satisfaction; the priest gives sacramental absolution. Contrition includes sorrow for sin and a purpose of amendment; imperfect contrition disposes us to receive forgiveness in the sacrament.

3. Mortal sin requires grave matter, full knowledge and deliberate consent. The sacrament restores grace lost through mortal sin, reconciles with the Church and strengthens conversion; regular confession of venial sins is recommended.

## Signs celebration and preparation

Only a priest with the required faculty absolves. Outline a private examination, greeting and sign of the cross, confession, counsel and penance, act of contrition and absolution, followed by carrying out the penance. Grave sins remembered after examination are confessed in kind and number as far as possible; honest forgetfulness differs from deliberate concealment. Explain the absolute sacramental seal and distinguish it from the limited confidentiality of an ordinary group discussion. Sources: CCC 1422–1498 [R22].

## Read and discover

Luke 15:11–32; John 20:19–23; Q14 Francis on the Father's mercy.

## Questions worth asking

### Why confess to a priest?

Christ's forgiveness is given through his minister and visibly reconciles us with the Church.

### What if I cannot remember the words?

Tell the priest; he can guide you.

### What if I repeatedly fear I have confessed badly?

Seek a consistent, trusted confessor and appropriate support; the course should not encourage repetitive checking or detailed public examinations.

## Reflect and practise

Spend 5 minutes reading a fictional “I have not been for years” dialogue, 5 arranging the steps on a card and 5 explaining what a penitent can ask for. Expected output: a private preparation guide. No one plays a confessor or gives mock absolution. Exit check: name the three conditions for mortal sin.

## Church precepts

Explain the obligation to confess grave sins at least annually once bound by the precept; distinguish that minimum from the recommendation of regular confession. Connect the Eucharistic precept with Session 16 and the summary in Session 22 (CCC 2042).

## Carry it into the week

Make a gentle private examen; arrange a conversation or confession if ready and eligible. Reconciliation with God does not remove obligations to repair harm or seek justice.
